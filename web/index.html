Why a lab and not a summary

Most students never get a lab for most of the rules they are taught. “For small angles, sin x is x.” Small compared to what? Here the student predicts first, then drags the slider and watches the rule hold, and then stop holding at a number the physics decides.

The model never says whether a rule is right. It finds the rule and writes the lab. A plain-code physics library decides the verdict and checks that every lab computes the real physics before anyone sees it. How the check works.
